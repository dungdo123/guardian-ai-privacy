AKT Guardian AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

AKT Guardian AI analyzes surveillance video displayed on your Windows screen. Image analysis occurs locally on your device. AKT Mobile LLC does not receive your surveillance images, alarm snapshots, or application settings unless you separately choose to provide them to us, for example in a support request.

1. Who we are

AKT Guardian AI ("Guardian" or the "App") is provided by AKT Mobile LLC, operating under the AKT Vision brand ("AKT," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how Guardian accesses, processes, stores, and transmits information.

2. Information Guardian accesses and processes

When monitoring is running, Guardian may access and process:

  • Pixels visible on the monitor or VMS window selected by the user.
  • Surveillance imagery that may include people, faces, vehicles, property, timestamps, camera labels, and other information visible in the video.
  • Detected event information, such as event type, confidence, camera label, and event time.
  • Application settings, including selected monitor, VMS choice, model/core, thresholds, and alarm preferences.
  • If Telegram notifications are enabled, the Telegram bot token and chat ID supplied by the user.

Guardian does not require an AKT account and does not include AKT advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics telemetry in the current version.

3. How information is used

The App uses this information only to:

  • identify camera regions shown by the selected VMS or screen;
  • detect configured surveillance events;
  • show alarms and notification counts;
  • save alarm evidence locally; and
  • send optional notifications requested by the user.

4. Local processing and storage

AI inference is performed locally on the Windows device. Guardian does not upload screen or surveillance imagery to AKT servers for inference. Settings and alarm snapshots are stored under the current Windows user's local application-data directory, normally:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\AKT Vision\Guardian AI

Alarm files remain on the device until the user deletes them. Users can delete stored alarm images and application settings through Windows File Explorer. Uninstalling the App may not automatically remove files in this local data directory.

5. Optional Telegram notifications

Telegram integration is disabled by default. If the user enables it and supplies Telegram credentials, Guardian communicates directly with api.telegram.org. Depending on the event, it may transmit the Telegram bot token, chat ID, status or alarm text, and alarm images. AKT does not receive those transmissions.

Telegram independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy, potentially in other countries. Users control this sharing and can stop it at any time by disabling Telegram notifications and removing the bot token and chat ID in Guardian. Users may also delete messages using Telegram's available controls. See the Telegram Privacy Policy.

6. Information shared with AKT

Guardian does not automatically send personal information, surveillance footage, alarm evidence, or settings to AKT. We receive information only when a user voluntarily contacts us or provides files for support, diagnostics, model training, or another requested service. We use such information to answer the request and provide the agreed service. Terms for a separate custom model-training engagement may be documented in an additional agreement.

We do not sell personal information obtained through Guardian.

7. Security

Guardian keeps its primary processing and evidence storage on the user's device. Access to local data depends on the security of the Windows account, device, VMS, Telegram account, and network. Users should protect their device, restrict access to the local data directory, keep software updated, and keep Telegram credentials confidential. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. User choices and data access

Users can:

  • start or stop monitoring at any time;
  • choose which monitor, VMS, event types, and notification options to use;
  • leave Telegram disabled or remove its credentials;
  • view or delete locally stored settings and alarm evidence; and
  • contact AKT about information voluntarily provided to us.

9. Surveillance operator responsibilities

The person or organization operating Guardian determines which surveillance video is monitored and is responsible for having a lawful basis, providing required notices, controlling access, establishing retention periods, and complying with applicable surveillance, employment, biometric, and data protection laws. Guardian should not be used where monitoring or recording is prohibited.

10. Children's privacy

Guardian is a surveillance and safety tool intended for use by adults and organizations. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Surveillance operators remain responsible for legal requirements relating to images of minors.
